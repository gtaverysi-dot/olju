About OnlineJobs University

OnlineJobs University is the education initiative of OnlineJobs.ph.

Who We Are

OnlineJobs.ph

OnlineJobs.ph helps Filipino workers connect directly with employers around the world.

OnlineJobs University

OnlineJobs University builds on that experience, turning lessons learned from years of online work into education for Filipino workers.

Our Story Starts With OnlineJobs.ph

The story behind OnlineJobs.ph began before its launch in 2009.

2009OnlineJobs.ph launches

  1. 1

    Where It All Began

    John Jonas, co-founder of OnlineJobs.ph, was running his own online business when he began hiring Filipino workers to take on tasks he couldn't manage alone.

  2. 2

    When His Team Stepped Up

    Then a serious family situation forced John to step away from much of the day-to-day work. He trusted his Filipino team to keep things moving while he focused on his family.

    When he returned, he found that they had done far more than follow instructions. They had kept the business running, solved problems, and taken responsibility without him directing every decision.

  3. 3

    What John Learned

    That experience changed how John saw his team and what Filipino workers could accomplish.

    He realized they were capable of far more when they were trusted, taught, and given room to think.

    That belief helped shape OnlineJobs.ph, which launched in 2009 to help employers hire Filipino workers directly and Filipino workers find long-term online opportunities.

What Years of Online Work Taught Us

Hiring someone is only the beginning of a working relationship.

Through years of working with our own Filipino team, we’ve seen what helps people grow after they’re hired.

Understand the Bigger Picture

Workers need to understand what their employer is trying to accomplish, not just the tasks they’ve been assigned.

Communicate and Solve Problems

Asking questions at the right time, communicating clearly, and solving problems as they arise all make a difference in day-to-day work.

Build Trust Through Responsibility

Good judgment and a willingness to take responsibility build trust. These qualities grow through experience, guidance, and opportunities to take on greater responsibility.

Knowing how to use a tool or complete a task is only part of what makes someone effective at online work.

These lessons helped shape the thinking behind OnlineJobs University.

OnlineJobs University Is the Next Chapter

OnlineJobs University carries the OnlineJobs.ph mission forward through education.

Educate. Elevate. Earn.

Our goal is to help Filipino workers learn from professionals with years of firsthand experience.

Through that education, we want them to build the confidence and judgment to handle challenges, take on greater responsibility, and grow in their online careers.
