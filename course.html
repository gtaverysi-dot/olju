What You’ll Learn (The Core Outcomes)

  • Navigate time zone differences, language barriers, and cultural differences to build a good working relationship with a foreign boss.
  • Execute daily reports effectively to communicate your progress, document your work, and solve problems without relying on time trackers.
  • Manage the administrative side of online work, including entering into contracts, setting up payment platforms, sending invoices, and figuring out holiday pay and leave.
  • Handle constructive feedback professionally and ask questions without fear to improve your work quality.
  • Develop career growth strategies by learning how to ask for a raise, negotiate tasks you do or don’t like, and manage multiple clients.

Course Description

Transitioning from a traditional 9-to-5 office job to an online career can be intimidating, and many find the prospect of working for a foreign boss terrifying due to cultural stereotypes or insecurities about using English. This unfamiliar territory often brings up anxieties about how communication will work, when to take breaks, or if you will actually get paid for the work.

This course eases those anxieties by giving you the information you need to prepare for working with a foreign boss. It teaches you to push preconceptions aside and focuses on practical communication strategies, showing you that building a successful online career ultimately depends on how well you work together.

The course covers 16 essential topics to guide you through your online work journey. You will learn how to handle daily reports, manage time tracking, get paid, request time off, navigate Philippine and foreign holidays, and take the initiative to grow your career.

Requirements / Recommended Experience

  • Your own computer
  • Reliable internet access

Who This Course Is For

Filipino online workers and freelancers, especially those who are new to remote work, who want to overcome the intimidation of working for a foreign employer and build a successful online career.
